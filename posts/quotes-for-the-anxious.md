Some quotes have been stuck on my head lately. Most of them are from TV series or movies I've watched in the past, and they kind of just stuck.

## 1. "Take a break from worrying about what you can't control."

Sometimes you just worry about things you have no say in; The impression of others about you, how something you've done will turn out, or even what might happen next month.

Sometimes it helps me to ask things like _"can I do something about it today?"_

- If so, do it. Do what you can about it, then take a break from thinking about it.
- Otherwise, it goes on my "not mine to carry" pile, at least for now.

It doesn't make the worry vanish. It just gives help me set it down for a while.

## 2. "Give yourself some credit."

I'm quick to notice what I haven't done and slow to notice what I have.
I assume I'm not alone here.
Many software engineers (myself included) probably don't credit themselves enough for what they've done.

So here's a small habit: at the end of the day, just think about what you've done, and what you're proud to have accomplished.
It doesn't have to be a lot, it doesn't have to be a huge accomplishment. One step goes a long way.

Try to stop thinking about everything that went wrong this past week, give yourself some credit.

## 3. "There is no tomorrow."

This is a quote is especially meaningful to me. I'm a big Rocky fan, and this scene has always just stuck with me.

It's easy to tell yourself you'll do something tomorrow. You'll start tomorrow. The small side project you have, you'll leave it for tomorrow.
There is no tomorrow.

I don't read this as gloomy. To me, it's actually the opposite. It's a reminder that you have full control.
You are the one who makes the decisions that affect your life. If you don't make the effort to take a step, you won't move out of place.

Right now, I only have today, this hour, and this next small task.

That's usually enough to start with.
